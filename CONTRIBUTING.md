# Contributing to Teknoloji an Kreyòl

Thank you for helping make technical education clearer and more useful in Haitian Creole.

## Best ways to help now

- Report an accuracy, language, accessibility, safety, privacy, or broken-link issue.
- Suggest a learner question with context: who needs it and what they should be able to do afterward.
- Review a specific Kreyòl term or explanation and describe regional/context differences.
- Point to a current primary or authoritative source.
- Volunteer as a Kreyòl, IT, education, or accessibility reviewer.

## Release 1 boundary

The first 30 topics and their order are controlled by the Release 1 baseline. New ideas are welcome, but most will enter the future backlog rather than interrupt the 30-day challenge. Critical accuracy, privacy, security, rights, and safety issues can always interrupt.

## How to report a correction

Include:

1. Episode ID and exact wording/time if published.
2. What appears wrong, unclear, inaccessible, or risky.
3. The corrected explanation in your own words.
4. A primary/authoritative source or linguistic context when possible.
5. Whether the issue may cause harm or expose private information.

Never paste a real password, token, private message, personal address, recovery code, or unredacted private screenshot into an issue.

## Language contributions

Haitian Creole usage can vary by speaker, region, discipline, and audience. Explain the context for a recommendation rather than declaring another speaker illegitimate. The project may retain an English technical term on screen while explaining it naturally in Kreyòl.

The MIT-Haiti STEM glossary is a useful starting input and describes itself as dynamic rather than prescriptive. Material disagreement is recorded when it affects meaning or trust.

## Technical contributions

- Prefer standards, official documentation, current government guidance, or the original vendor's documentation.
- Open and read the source; do not submit a search-result snippet as evidence.
- Label version-, platform-, region-, or jurisdiction-specific claims.
- State important limitations and counterexamples.
- Never include live malware, malicious links, real credentials, or destructive steps.

## Pull requests and rights

The repository's long-term content/code license has not yet been selected. Until that decision is recorded, please use Issues for substantive content proposals. Small maintenance pull requests may be reviewed, but acceptance does not imply that the repository content has an open-source or open-content license.

## Review process

1. Triage against scope and severity.
2. Verify the issue and source.
3. Complete creator review and consult an external contributor when available or necessary.
4. Record a correction or decision.
5. Update the brief, public content, or future backlog.
6. Credit a contributor only with their permission.

## Conduct

Be specific, patient, and respectful. Critique content and evidence, not a person's intelligence, accent, location, or beginner status. Harassment, credential requests, scams, impersonation, and unsafe remote-access offers are not accepted.
