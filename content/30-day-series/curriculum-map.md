# 30-day curriculum map

## Audience hypothesis

Haitian Creole-speaking beginners aged roughly 16 and older who use a smartphone or computer but want a clearer mental model of IT, safer digital habits, practical troubleshooting skills, or an introduction to IT careers. This remains a hypothesis until sponsor confirmation and post-challenge learner review.

## Release-level learning outcomes

After the series, a learner should be able to:

1. distinguish hardware, software, operating systems, files, applications, data, and storage;
2. use a simple, repeatable troubleshooting process and communicate a useful support request;
3. explain at a beginner level how devices, local networks, the internet, web services, DNS, IP addresses, and cloud services relate;
4. take basic steps to protect accounts, devices, and data without believing any step guarantees safety;
5. name several IT work areas and choose one safe next practice path.

These are intended outcomes. Actual comprehension will be checked only after video 30.

## Curriculum design rules

- One observable objective per episode.
- Foundations before mechanisms; mechanisms before protection; protection before career choice.
- Concrete phone/home/work examples before abstract vocabulary.
- Primarily Haitian Creole audio, with useful English technical labels on screen.
- No real credentials, personal data, risky downloads, or destructive commands.
- Each brief labels source, risk, hook, visual, and recall/application prompt before scripting.

## Sequencing logic

- E01–E07 establish vocabulary used everywhere else.
- E08–E11 teach disciplined observation and support communication before networking concepts.
- E12–E20 build a system model from internet/web through identity.
- E21–E27 apply that model to everyday risk without fear-based promises.
- E28–E30 connect skills to work and a next action.

## Post-Day-30 comprehension sample

Before results are viewed, select a balanced six-video sample by fixed IDs: **E02, E08, E13, E17, E22, and E29**. This prevents choosing only apparent winners. One plain recall or application question per sampled video will be finalized before Day 1 and administered after Day 30 under the measurement plan.

## PM lesson: curriculum as product scope

The curriculum map describes learner capability, not merely 30 titles. That lets quality reviewers ask whether an episode contributes to an outcome and lets change control reject an exciting topic that would weaken the intended sequence.
