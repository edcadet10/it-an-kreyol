# E18 — Kisa “cloud” la ye?

## Learning objective

The learner can explain cloud computing as on-demand network access to shared configurable computing resources/services and distinguish it from a file floating nowhere.

## Audience problem

“It is in the cloud” can hide where data is processed, who operates the service, what depends on connectivity, and which responsibilities remain with the user.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “Cloud la pa nan syèl la—se resous enfòmatik yon founisè mete disponib atravè rezo.”
- Give a photo-storage or webmail example.
- Explain remote provider-operated servers/storage/apps and on-demand network access.
- Mention tradeoffs: convenience/scaling versus connectivity, provider, privacy, account, and cost dependencies.
- Do not teach SaaS/PaaS/IaaS taxonomy in this short episode unless it remains one objective.

## Visual

Phone ↔ network ↔ provider data-center icon; show that a local copy and provider copy may differ.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Si yon fichye nan cloud, èske sa vle di li pa sou okenn òdinatè fizik?”

## Evidence

- [NIST SP 800-145 — The Definition of Cloud Computing](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/145/final)

## Review flags

- Translate the concept naturally; do not force all NIST definition clauses into spoken copy.
- Avoid “someone else's computer” as the entire definition; it omits the service model and shared/on-demand characteristics.
