# E26 — App permissions

## Learning objective

The learner can review an app's camera, microphone, contacts, files/photos, and location permissions and grant only access needed for the feature being used.

## Audience problem

People often tap “Allow” automatically and forget that permissions can usually be reviewed or changed later.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “Poukisa yon app senp ap mande contacts, microphone, ak location tout tan?”
- A permission lets an app use a device capability or data category.
- Ask whether the access is necessary for the intended feature and whether “only while using” or selected-item access is available.
- Android and iOS wording/paths vary; show one current version and tell viewers to use their official device help.
- Removing permission can limit a feature; it is a tradeoff, not proof the app is malicious.

## Visual

Sanitized permission list on a test device/account; no real contacts, photos, maps, or notifications visible.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Si yon app pa bezwen microphone pou fonksyon w ap itilize a, ki prensip ki ede w deside? Bay sèlman aksè ki nesesè.”

## Evidence

- [Android Help — Change app permissions](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9431959)
- [Apple Support — Control what you share on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-lamr/guide/iphone/iph6e7d349d1/ios)
- [NIST — Least privilege](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/least_privilege)

## Review flags

- Privacy/security review required.
- Record exact OS/version; never imply paths or permission behavior are identical.
