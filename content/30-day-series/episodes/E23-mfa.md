# E23 — MFA ajoute yon lòt baryè

## Learning objective

The learner can explain that multifactor authentication uses more than one factor and can protect an account when a password is compromised, while recognizing that methods differ in phishing resistance.

## Audience problem

People may confuse two steps with two independent factors or believe any MFA makes an account impossible to steal.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “Yon moun vòlè modpas ou. Ki lòt bagay ki ka toujou bloke l?”
- Factors are commonly something known, possessed, or inherent; use careful, current language.
- MFA combines factors; two passwords are not two different factors.
- Authenticator apps, security keys/passkeys, push, SMS, and biometrics differ in operation and risk.
- Turn on the strongest practical method each important service supports and secure recovery options.

## Visual

Password barrier plus possession/biometric barrier. Do not show a real MFA seed, QR code, recovery code, or approval prompt.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Yon modpas ak yon lòt kestyon sekrè: èske sa nesesèman de faktè diferan?”

## Evidence

- [NIST — Multi-Factor Authentication](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber/guidance-topic/multi-factor-authentication)
- [NIST SP 800-63B-4](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html)

## Review flags

- Security reviewer must check current phishing-resistance terminology.
- Do not rank methods beyond what the cited guidance directly supports.
