# E30 — Chwazi pwochen etap ou nan IT

## Learning objective

The learner can select one safe beginner practice track and create one small evidence artifact rather than collecting more disconnected advice.

## Audience problem

After a broad introduction, learners can feel overwhelmed and jump among tutorials without completing anything demonstrable.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “Apre 30 jou, pwochen etap la se pa gade 300 lòt videyo—se chwazi yon ti bagay pou w pratike.”
- Support track: write a sanitized problem ticket and troubleshooting record.
- Networking track: draw a home-network diagram without publishing real addresses/passwords.
- Security track: audit update/MFA/backup status without exposing account details.
- Systems/data track: make a device/file inventory using invented or private data.
- Choose one, complete it, explain what was learned, and decide the next small step.

## Visual

Four path cards converge on `fè → dokimante → esplike → amelyore`.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Ekri yon sèl chemen ou chwazi ak yon ti prèv ou pral kreye. Pa poste okenn enfòmasyon prive.”
