# E08 — Yon metòd pou rezoud pwoblèm

## Learning objective

The learner can apply a safe beginner loop: observe, reproduce/define scope, form a simple hypothesis, change one reversible thing, test, and document.

## Audience problem

Randomly changing many settings can hide the cause, create new problems, and make support harder.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “Lè teknoloji pa mache, pa peze tout bouton yo an menm tan.”
- Observe: exact symptom and message; when did it start?
- Scope: one app, one device, one account, or everyone?
- Change one low-risk, reversible thing based on a reason.
- Test the same action again; record result; undo if worse.
- Escalate rather than guessing when money, accounts, safety, or important data are at risk.

## Visual

Five-card loop with a charger/cable example that uses no account or destructive action.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Poukisa nou chanje yon sèl bagay alafwa?”

## Evidence

## Review flags

- Cisco sources use network/product contexts; the beginner loop is a tailored synthesis, not a quoted universal standard.
- Never use “restart” as a universal fix; it is one possible reversible test after observing.
