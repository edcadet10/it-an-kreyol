# E15 — Kisa yon IP address ye?

## Learning objective

The learner can describe an IP address as a network-layer identifier used to deliver packets, while recognizing that private/public addresses, sharing, translation, and reassignment make a home-address analogy incomplete.

## Audience problem

Beginners may believe an IP address uniquely and permanently identifies one person or exact physical location.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “IP address sanble ak yon adrès pou trafik rezo—men li pa kat idantite pèsonèl ou.”
- Devices/interfaces use IP addresses so network traffic can be routed.
- IPv4 and IPv6 have different forms; show documentation ranges only.
- Home devices commonly have private addresses while a router/provider exposes or shares a public-facing address.
- Addresses can change and do not, by themselves, prove a person's identity or exact location.

## Visual

Use reserved example addresses such as `192.0.2.10` and `2001:db8::10`, subject to source verification; never show the creator's real address.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Èske yon IP address pou kont li pwouve kiyès ki te sèvi ak yon aparèy? Non.”
