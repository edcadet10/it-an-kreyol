# E02 — Hardware ak software

## Learning objective

The learner can classify a physical computer component as hardware and a program/instruction set as software.

## Audience problem

People often use “computer” for both the physical device and everything running on it, which makes later troubleshooting confusing.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “Si w ka manyen li, èske se hardware? E app TikTok la menm—ki sa li ye?”
- Point to phone screen, camera, keyboard, or charger as physical examples.
- Show an app icon and explain software as programs/instructions that direct the hardware.
- Explain that neither category is useful alone: software needs hardware to run; hardware needs instructions to do useful work.
- Keep the familiar English labels on screen and complete a separate creator Kreyòl-language pass for the explanations.

## Visual

Split screen: `Hardware — pati fizik` and `Software — pwogram/enstriksyon`; move four example cards into the right side.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Yon sourit se hardware. Microsoft Word se kisa?”

## Evidence

- [IBM — What is computer hardware?](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/hardware)

## Review flags

- Avoid saying “anything you can touch is hardware” without limiting it to components/devices in the system.
- Confirm natural usage of `pati fizik`, `pyès`, `materyèl`, and `lojisyèl/software`.
