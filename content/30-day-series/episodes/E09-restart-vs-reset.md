# E09 — Restart pa menm ak reset

## Learning objective

The learner can distinguish restarting/power-cycling from a reset operation and will check device-specific consequences before selecting any reset that may erase settings or data.

## Audience problem

Similar words in menus can lead a beginner to erase network settings, app state, or an entire device while attempting a simple restart.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “De bouton sa yo sanble, men youn ka fèmen epi relouvri aparèy la; lòt la ka efase bagay.”
- Restart/reboot normally stops and starts the operating session; unsaved work can still be lost.
- “Reset” is not one universal action: network reset, app reset, settings reset, and factory reset have different consequences.
- Read the confirmation screen and official device instructions; back up important data before destructive reset.
- If account recovery information is missing, stop and get trusted help.

## Visual

Use drawn/mock menus, not a live destructive screen. Highlight `Restart` versus several possible `Reset...` labels.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Si w wè `factory reset`, èske w dwe peze li pou teste vit? Non—tcheke sa l ap efase epi fè backup anvan.”

## Evidence

- Current official documentation for the exact demonstrated Android/iOS/Windows device is required before `Ready`.

## Review flags

- High-risk technical and privacy approval required.
- Do not perform a factory reset in the video or imply all restart/reset behavior is identical.
