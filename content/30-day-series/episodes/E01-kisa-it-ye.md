# E01 — Kisa IT ye?

## Learning objective

The learner can explain that information technology uses computers, software, networks, and data to create, process, store, secure, and share information—and that IT is broader than repair.

## Audience problem

Many beginners hear “IT” and picture only a person fixing a laptop. That makes the field and its career paths feel narrower than they are.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “Si w voye yon mesaj, peye ak telefòn ou, oswa konekte ak Wi‑Fi jodi a, ou deja sèvi ak IT. Men, kisa IT ye?”
- Connect four daily examples to devices, software, networks, and information.
- Give the English label `Information Technology (IT)` and the Kreyòl explanation `teknoloji enfòmasyon` without claiming one label is universally preferred.
- State clearly: IT includes support, systems, networks, security, cloud, software, and data; it is not only repair.
- Preview that the next 29 days will build the map one piece at a time.

## Visual

Four quick objects/icons: phone, app, Wi‑Fi/router, and a file/database. Join them with the word `enfòmasyon`.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Nan twa bagay sa yo—WhatsApp, yon router, ak yon dosye foto—ki ladan yo IT manyen? Repons lan: tout twa. Ki pati ou pi anvi konprann?”
