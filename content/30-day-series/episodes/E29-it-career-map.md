# E29 — IT pa yon sèl metye

## Learning objective

The learner can compare several broad IT work areas and choose one to investigate based on tasks and interests rather than salary hype.

## Audience problem

“Work in IT” is too broad to guide learning; viral career claims often ignore location, experience, education, and labor-market change.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “Si w di `mwen vle travay nan IT`, se tankou ou di `mwen vle travay nan lasante`—gen anpil wòl diferan.”
- Support: help users and resolve incidents.
- Networking/systems/cloud: connect, operate, and maintain services/infrastructure.
- Security: reduce, monitor, and respond to risk.
- Software/data: build applications and work with data; roles overlap and names vary.
- Choose by tasks you want to practice; verify local requirements and openings.

## Visual

Four or five branches with one task and one beginner artifact each. Avoid ranking branches.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Ki aktivite ki plis atire w: ede itilizatè, konekte sistèm, pwoteje yo, oswa konstwi/analize?”

## Evidence

- [BLS — Computer and Information Technology Occupations](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/) — U.S.-specific classification and data
- Local Haiti/diaspora labor-market sources are required before making geographic demand/pay claims.

## Review flags

- No “fastest path,” guaranteed salary, or universal degree requirement.
- Career taxonomy is simplified and overlapping.
