# E10 — Lè storage prèske plen

## Learning objective

The learner can inspect storage categories, protect important files, and choose a targeted cleanup instead of deleting at random.

## Audience problem

A “storage full” warning can trigger panic deletion of photos, downloads, or app data without a recoverable copy.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “Telefòn oswa òdinatè w di storage plen? Premye etap la se pa efase o aza.”
- Inspect which categories use space: apps, photos/video, downloads, temporary items, system/reserved.
- Back up irreplaceable items and verify the copy before deletion.
- Start with known, reviewable items; explain that clearing app data can sign users out or remove offline content.
- Leave OS-specific steps to a clearly labeled demo version.

## Visual

Sanitized storage-category chart with fictional values; show “inspect → backup → verify → clean.”

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Anvan ou efase foto enpòtan, ki de bagay ou dwe fè? Fè yon kopi epi verifye kopi a.”

## Evidence

- [Microsoft Support — Storage settings in Windows](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/storage-filemanagement/storage-settings-in-windows)
- Official Android/iOS storage guidance is required if a phone UI is demonstrated.

## Review flags

- Never show real photos, account names, or recent files.
- Avoid promising that cleanup fixes every performance problem.
