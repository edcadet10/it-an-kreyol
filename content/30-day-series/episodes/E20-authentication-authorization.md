# E20 — Authentication ak authorization

## Learning objective

The learner can distinguish proving/establishing confidence in identity (authentication) from deciding which resources/actions that identity may access (authorization).

## Audience problem

The similar English words are often confused, which makes account permissions and security controls harder to understand.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “Montre kat idantite w pa vle di ou gen kle pou tout pòt.”
- Authentication answers, roughly, “Can the system establish that this account/user controls the expected authenticator?”
- Authorization answers, “What may this identity do or access?”
- Login can succeed while access to an admin page is denied; that is not a contradiction.
- Bridge to E22/E23: passwords and MFA are authentication tools, not universal permission.

## Visual

Checkpoint verifies fictional identity, then a separate colored badge controls two room permissions.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Ou antre nan account lan, men ou pa ka efase dosye ekip la. Ki pati refize aksyon an: authentication oswa authorization?”
