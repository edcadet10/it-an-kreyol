# E28 — Sa help desk fè

## Learning objective

The learner can describe user-support work as listening, documenting, diagnosing, guiding, resolving or escalating, and recognizing recurring problems—not merely “fixing computers.”

## Audience problem

Beginners may not see the communication, documentation, and systematic reasoning in entry-level support work.

## Draft beats

- **Hook draft:** “Bon help desk pa kòmanse ak repons lan; li kòmanse ak bon kestyon.”
- Connect to E11's useful request and E08's troubleshooting loop.
- Technician confirms impact/scope, collects evidence, tries safe steps, documents the result, and escalates when needed.
- Support may cover people, software, devices, accounts, and networks.
- Entry paths vary; do not promise a job, credential, or salary.

## Visual

Fictional ticket moves through intake → diagnose → guide/fix → verify → document/escalate.

## Knowledge check / CTA

“Ki de konpetans ki enpòtan ansanm nan help desk: teknoloji ak kisa? Kominikasyon/dokimantasyon.”
