# Release 1 content backlog

## Backlog policy

The product owner owns ordering. During the 30-day challenge, the order is fixed unless a safety/accuracy dependency forces an emergency change. Performance signals do not reorder work until after Gate 2.

## Status vocabulary

`Briefed → Ready → Script → Record → Edit → QA → Scheduled → Published → Measured`

All items begin as `Briefed`. They are not `Ready` until direct sources, Kreyòl questions, hook, example, visual, and check/CTA pass Definition of Ready.

- [ ] No audience analytics are added until E30 is public.
- [ ] Gate 2 and Gate 3 decisions are documented.
