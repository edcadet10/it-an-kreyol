# Release 1 series style guide

## Working identity

- Name: **Teknoloji an Kreyòl**
- Working promise: **Teknoloji klè. An kreyòl. Pou tout moun.**
- Episode label: `Jou 01/30 · E01`
- Tone: warm, direct, curious, practical, never condescending or fear-based
- Presenter posture: a guide who explains and verifies, not an infallible guru

Brand name, handle, colors, type, and avatar remain sponsor decisions. Do not spend critical-path time on a complex identity before the first seven videos are ready.

## Default episode pattern

1. **Hook:** reveal a problem, misconception, or useful surprise.
2. **Promise:** state what the learner will understand/do.
3. **Explain:** one idea in plain Kreyòl.
4. **Show:** one safe concrete example or visual model.
5. **Limit:** state the important caveat when the simple model could mislead.
6. **Check/CTA:** ask one recall/application question or invite the next learning choice.

This is a default, not a platform law. Hook styles and visuals are pre-labeled so they can be compared cautiously after Day 30.

## Language pattern

- Speak primarily in natural Haitian Creole.
- Put the common English term on screen on first use: `sistèm operasyon (operating system / OS)`.
- Reuse the simplest chosen term consistently within the episode.
- Prefer a short explanation over an unfamiliar invented translation.
- Add disputed or evolving terms to `data/terminology-log.csv`.
- Complete a separate creator language/caption check of spoken and written text; invite sourced external feedback when available.

## Visual pattern

- Vertical composition.
- One title, one diagram/example, and one takeaway at a time.
- Keep critical words away from likely interface overlays; verify in current upload preview.
- Use high contrast and readable type; do not communicate a distinction by color alone.
- Avoid tiny screenshots; crop to the single safe detail being explained.
- Use fictional accounts, domains, addresses, tickets, and messages.

## Audio and captions

- Voice must be clear on an ordinary phone speaker.
- Background sound cannot compete with instruction.
- Every spoken instructional line receives a manually reviewed caption.
- Captions preserve meaning rather than blindly preserving recognition errors.
- Technical terms, acronyms, names, and numbers receive an explicit second check.

## Caption-copy pattern

```text
Jou XX/30 — [plain-language topic]

[One-sentence takeaway in Kreyòl.]

Kesyon: [recall/application prompt]

Sous yo nan pwojè GitHub la. [Only add a working route/link when available.]
```

Hashtags remain a pre-launch account/search decision. Do not promise that a hashtag guarantees distribution.

## File naming

- Brief: `EXX-short-slug.md`
- Script: `EXX-script-v01.md`
- Master: `EXX-master-v01-YYYYMMDD.mp4`
- Captions: `EXX-captions-v01.srt`
- Thumbnail/cover: `EXX-cover-v01.png`

Raw/media files stay in private storage, not Git.

## Comment posture

- Thank specific corrections and verify before accepting them.
- Never request credentials, payment, or remote access.
- Do not diagnose a high-risk personal situation in comments.
- Remove/report scams, impersonation, harassment, and exposed private data.
- During Days 1–30, moderate and correct as needed but do not count, code, log, or use performance feedback; the only recorded project datum is posted yes/no.
