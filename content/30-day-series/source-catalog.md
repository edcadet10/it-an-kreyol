## Source-selection rules

1. Prefer the current standard, official documentation, government guidance, or original vendor documentation.
2. For interface steps, record operating system/app version and check on the device used in the demo.
3. For cybersecurity, recheck the source immediately before recording.
4. A vendor learning page may explain a general concept, but remove marketing and vendor-specific claims.
5. Career data from BLS describe the United States; do not present them as Haiti or global labor-market facts.
6. Wikipedia, search snippets, AI output, and unsourced social posts may generate questions but do not approve a factual claim.
7. Every final script carries direct links, not only source IDs.
