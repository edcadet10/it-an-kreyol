# Stakeholder and communication plan

## Stakeholder register

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Needs | Engagement approach |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Sponsor / creator | High | High | A manageable plan, creative ownership, visible progress, honest evidence | Manage closely; owns priority and publish decisions |
| Haitian Creole beginner viewers | High | Medium collectively | Clear, respectful, useful explanations and accessible captions | Test wording, invite questions, review feedback after Day 30 |
| Kreyòl reviewer | High | High on language quality | Context, enough review time, visible term decisions | Consult before recording or before final edit |
| IT accuracy reviewer | High | High on technical quality | Claims, sources, demo steps, clear deadline | Consult before scheduling high-risk content |
| Haitian educators / community contributors | Medium–High | Medium | Reusable material, respectful pedagogy, attribution | Invite corrections and curriculum feedback |
| TikTok | Medium | High | Policy-compliant, safe content | Monitor official platform guidance and account notices |
| GitHub visitors / recruiters | Medium | Low on delivery | Clear evidence of PM thinking and honest outcomes | Maintain navigation, decision trail, and release summary |
| Future sponsors or partners | Unknown | Potentially High | Credibility, audience evidence, clear rights | Out of scope until after Gate 3 |

No viewer is treated as a research participant merely because they comment. Direct interviews, testimonials, or identifiable quotes require clear permission.

## Roles and decision rights

| Role | Responsibilities | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsor / product owner | Vision, audience, priority, budget, risk acceptance, final content approval | Final decision on baselines, material changes, publish, continue/pause |
| Project manager | Plan, workflow, status, dependencies, risks, gates, repository | Recommends action; executes approved standard changes |
| Creator / presenter | Research, script, perform, edit, publish, moderate | Controls creative execution within baseline |
| Kreyòl reviewer | Naturalness, spelling, pronunciation, captions, cultural clarity | May block language approval |
| IT reviewer | Accuracy, currency, safe demos, source sufficiency | May block technical approval |
| AI assistant | Research support, drafting, structure, checks, explanations | No independent approval or publication authority |
| Viewer/advisor | Relevance, comprehension, questions, corrections | Advisory |

One human may hold several roles, but the approval criteria remain separate.

## RACI matrix

`R` = does the work, `A` = owns the result, `C` = consulted, `I` = informed.

| Deliverable / decision | Sponsor | PM | Creator | Kreyòl reviewer | IT reviewer | Viewers/advisors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Charter and baseline | A | R | C | C | C | I |
| Audience hypothesis | A | R | R | C | C | C |
| Curriculum sequence | A | R | R | C | C | C |
| Episode script | I | C | A/R | C | C | I |
| Language approval | I | I | C | A/R | I | C |
| Technical approval | I | I | C | I | A/R | C |
| Final publish decision | A | I | R | C | C | I |
| Emergency correction | A | R | R | C | C | I |
| Measurement analysis | A | R | R | C | C | I |
| Continue/adapt/pause | A | R | C | C | C | C |

## Communication cadence

| Communication | Participants | Timing | Format / record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flow check | Creator/PM | Daily, 5 minutes | GitHub issue status; no audience analytics Days 1–30 |
| Script/QA review | Creator + relevant reviewer | Before scheduling each post | Episode brief checklist / pull request or issue comment |
| Planning and risk review | Sponsor/PM/creator | Weekly, 20 minutes | Status and risk-register update |
| Process retrospective | Creator/PM | Weekly, 15 minutes | Retrospective template; mechanics only during blackout |
| Critical issue escalation | Sponsor + relevant reviewer | Immediately | Private contact first; public correction only after containment plan |
| Gate review | Sponsor and project team | G1, G2, G3 | Checklist, decision record, status update |
| Community update | Public | At meaningful milestones | README/status/release notes; never disclose private analytics or people |

## Communication rules

- Lead with decision, impact, owner, and due condition.
- Separate fact, assumption, interpretation, and decision.
- Write public artifacts as if a learner, collaborator, and recruiter will all read them.
- Do not publish names, messages, screenshots, or quotes from private conversations without permission.
- Do not let a reviewer silence the creator's authentic voice; reviewers identify issues and explain why.
- Disagreement over a technical or language term goes into the terminology/decision log rather than disappearing in chat.
- A critical safety or privacy concern is escalated immediately even during the measurement blackout.

## Feedback handling

During Days 1–30, comments may be read for moderation, safety, corrections, and respectful replies, but they are not counted, coded, or used to reprioritize the series. After Day 30, comments can be coded into questions, misconceptions, requests, corrections, and positive/negative sentiment using de-identified notes.

## PM lesson: stakeholder management is not broadcasting

Communication sends information. Engagement creates the conditions for people to influence the outcome appropriately. The learner, reviewer, platform, and sponsor need different information and different decision rights; one generic update would not serve them all.
