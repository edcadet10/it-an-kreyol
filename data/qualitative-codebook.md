# Post-Day-30 qualitative codebook

Do not code or count audience comments until video 30 is public. Safety moderation and factual correction still operate during the blackout.

| Code | Include | Exclude | Example, paraphrased |
|---|---|---|---|
| `QUESTION_CONCEPT` | A sincere question about the taught concept | Generic “what?” with no context; spam | Asks whether RAM stores photos after shutdown |
| `QUESTION_APPLY` | A question about applying the concept safely | Request for passwords, payment, private remote access | Asks how to identify the router in a combined gateway |
| `MISCONCEPTION` | A statement revealing a specific incorrect model | Mere disagreement without interpretable claim | Says HTTPS proves the business is legitimate |
| `APPLICATION_STORY` | Describes using/testing the lesson | Vague praise | Says they reviewed an app's microphone permission |
| `CORRECTION_VALID` | Supported factual/language/accessibility correction | Insult or preference without context | Notes a term is unnatural in a named usage context |
| `REQUEST_FUTURE` | Clear desired future capability/problem | Unrelated promotion | Requests a beginner subnetting explanation |
| `POSITIVE_GENERAL` | General positive response | Learning evidence | “Mwen renmen sa” |
| `NEGATIVE_GENERAL` | General negative response | Specific correction/question | “Sa pa bon” |
| `SPAM_SCAM_HARASSMENT` | Promotion, credential/payment request, impersonation, abuse | Good-faith critical feedback | Offers paid account recovery in private messages |

## Coding rules

- De-identify notes and do not quote a person publicly without permission.
- One comment may receive multiple relevant codes; document that counts can overlap.
- Seek a second reviewer for a small sample before strong qualitative claims; if unavailable, label the qualitative finding exploratory and creator-coded.
- Disagreement is resolved by discussion or reported as disagreement, not forced agreement.
- Counts support interpretation; they do not turn comments into a representative survey.
